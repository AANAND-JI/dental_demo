Dr Ruth Miller

Lead Oral Surgeon and Co‑Founder

Dr Ruth Miller co‑founded ToothWise and leads our surgical service. With over 18 years' clinical experience, she specialises in complex extractions, sedation and surgical planning delivered with calm, patient‑centred care.

Her approach—clear communication, careful planning and empathetic care—sets the tone for the whole clinic.
