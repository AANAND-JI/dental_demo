Comprehensive General Dentistry

General dentistry is the cornerstone of long‑term oral health. At ToothWise we focus on prevention, early detection and practical treatment plans that protect your smile for years to come.

From thorough examinations and professional cleanings to discreet, tooth‑coloured restorations and gum care, every appointment is calm, unhurried and tailored to your needs. We explain options clearly so you can choose with confidence.

Key Benefits

  • Early detection of decay, gum disease and other oral health concerns.
  • Professional hygiene appointments to remove plaque and calculus effectively.
  • Tooth‑coloured, biocompatible restorations that blend with your smile.
  • Clear, personalised advice to keep your smile healthy between visits.

Our Procedure

  1. Consultation & Exam. We review your history and carry out a gentle, thorough examination with digital imaging.
  2. Diagnosis & Plan. We explain our findings clearly and build a treatment plan around your goals and budget.
  3. Treatment. Cleaning, fillings or other care is carried out comfortably, at your pace.
  4. Prevention & Follow-up. We schedule your next check-up and share tailored home-care tips.
"Prevention is kinder — and more cost‑effective — than cure. A six‑month check is the single best step to protect your smile." — Dr Alex Turner
